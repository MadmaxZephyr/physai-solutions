Accelerating Industrial Innovation with AI

Engineering development cycles are still measured in years. The design spaces that matter most go unexplored. Not because the answers don't exist, but because finding them takes too long.

PhysicsX changes that. We embed physics AI directly into engineering workflows, compressing the distance between what you can imagine and what you can build.

1

Mission

We empower industrial organizations to build beyond human imagination and address the defining challenges of our time.

The next industrial revolution will be AI-native. PhysicsX is the platform at its center: not AI bolted onto existing toolchains, but AI as a core primitive of how engineering gets done. We're working with the organizations building the world's most complex systems to redefine what's possible in the time they have.
